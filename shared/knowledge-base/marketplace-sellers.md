# Marketplace Sellers

## What marketplace items are
Besides its own range, ByteCart lets independent marketplace sellers sell on bytecart.example. The product page and the order show "Sold by <seller name>" for these items; items sold by ByteCart show "Sold by ByteCart".
Marketplace sellers ship the items themselves, set their own shipping fees and delivery times, and provide the tracking ID. ByteCart always processes the payment, so you pay ByteCart even for marketplace items.
An order can contain both ByteCart items and marketplace items; they are shipped separately.

## Returns for marketplace items
Marketplace items follow the seller's own return policy, which is shown on the product page. Every seller must accept returns for at least 14 days after delivery (EU right of withdrawal).
The ByteCart Plus 60-day return window and the ByteCart free return label do not apply to marketplace items. The seller provides the return address and decides who pays return shipping, as stated in its policy.
Once the seller has received the returned item, it must issue the refund within 14 days.
Start a marketplace return in Account > Orders > "Contact seller".

## Contacting the seller
All communication with a seller goes through Account > Orders > "Contact seller", so ByteCart can see the conversation if you need help later.
Sellers are expected to answer within 2 business days.

## ByteCart Guarantee
The ByteCart Guarantee protects every marketplace purchase paid through ByteCart. You can open a Guarantee claim in Account > Orders if:
- the item has not arrived 3 business days after the estimated delivery date, or
- the item is significantly different from its description, damaged or faulty, or
- the seller has not refunded an accepted return within 14 days.
You must first contact the seller. If the seller has not answered within 3 days of your message, or you are not satisfied with the answer, you can open the claim.
Claims must be opened within 90 days of the estimated delivery date. The Guarantee covers the item price plus the original shipping cost, up to €2,500 per order.
ByteCart decides on a claim within 5 business days and, if approved, refunds you directly to the original payment method; ByteCart then recovers the money from the seller.

## Warranty for marketplace items
For marketplace items the seller is responsible for the 2-year legal guarantee. Contact the seller first; if the seller does not respond within 3 days, open a ByteCart Guarantee claim.
The ByteCart delay voucher and ByteCart Care extended warranty are not available for marketplace items.

## Seller ratings and reporting a seller
Every seller has a public rating from 1 to 5 stars based on verified purchases in the last 12 months. Only customers who bought from the seller can leave a rating, within 60 days of delivery.
If you suspect a counterfeit product or a fraudulent seller, use "Report seller" on the seller page. ByteCart reviews reports within 2 business days and can suspend the seller's listings during the investigation.

## Selling on ByteCart
Businesses registered in the EU can apply to become marketplace sellers. The seller plan costs €39 per month plus a commission of 8–15% of the item price depending on the category. Sellers must ship within 3 business days and keep a cancellation rate below 2.5%.
