# Shipping and Delivery

## Shipping options and fees
Standard delivery takes 2–4 business days and costs €4.99. Free shipping: standard delivery is free when you spend €39 or more on items sold by ByteCart. The €39 threshold only counts the ByteCart items in your cart; marketplace items in the same cart do not count and are shipped separately with the seller's own shipping fee.
Express delivery costs €9.99 and arrives the next business day if the order is placed before 14:00 CET, Monday to Friday. Orders placed after 14:00 or at the weekend are delivered on the second business day.
ByteCart Plus members get free standard delivery on every order with no minimum order value, and express delivery for €4.99.
Bulky items over 20 kg are delivered by a two-person team for a flat fee of €29 (also for Plus members) within 5–10 business days.
Fees and free-shipping thresholds apply only to items sold by ByteCart; marketplace sellers set their own shipping fees.

## Delivery countries
ByteCart delivers to 12 countries: Germany, Austria, France, the Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Ireland, Denmark and Sweden.
Deliveries to Portugal, Ireland and Sweden take 1–2 business days longer than the standard delivery times above.
ByteCart does not deliver to Switzerland, Norway or the United Kingdom, and does not ship to PO boxes. Parcel lockers are supported in Germany, the Netherlands and Belgium.

## Order statuses
Every order in Account > Orders has one of these statuses:
- processing: the order has been received and is being prepared; it can still be cancelled or changed.
- shipped: the parcel has been handed to the carrier and is on its way; the estimated delivery date is shown on the order.
- delivered: the carrier has confirmed delivery (delivery date shown on the order).
- delayed: the parcel has not been delivered by the estimated delivery date.
- cancelled: the order was cancelled before shipping and has been refunded in full.
- returned: the returned items have been received at the ByteCart warehouse and the refund has been issued.

## Carriers and tracking
ByteCart ships with DHL, DPD, GLS and PostNL. When the order is shipped you receive an email with the carrier and the tracking ID; the same information is in Account > Orders.
Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear on the carrier website after the order status changes to "shipped".
Orders with several items may be sent in more than one parcel, each with its own tracking ID.

## Processing time, cancelling and changing an order
Orders are usually shipped within 24 hours of being placed (business days).
You can cancel an order free of charge, or change the delivery address, only while its status is "processing". Once the status is "shipped" the order cannot be cancelled or redirected by ByteCart; you can refuse the delivery or use the carrier's redirection service, and the order is then handled as a return.
A cancelled order is refunded in full, including shipping fees; for card payments the authorisation hold is released by the bank (see Payments and Billing).

## Delayed orders
Your order still hasn't arrived by the date it was supposed to arrive? An order that is not delivered by its estimated delivery date gets the status "delayed". Once it is 3 business days past the estimated delivery date, you can ask support to open a carrier investigation, which takes up to 5 business days.
If the carrier investigation does not locate the parcel, or if there has been no tracking update for 10 business days after the estimated delivery date, the parcel is declared lost and you can choose between a free replacement (if in stock) or a full refund.
Delay compensation (delay voucher): when an order sold and shipped by ByteCart arrives late, 5 or more business days after the estimated delivery date, the customer receives a goodwill voucher on request: €5 for standard customers, €10 for ByteCart Plus members. The voucher is added as ByteCart credit. The delay voucher does not apply to marketplace seller shipments.

## Delivered but not received
If tracking says "delivered" but you never got the parcel and cannot find it, check with neighbours, your parcel locker and any safe place you selected, and wait 48 hours (carriers sometimes scan parcels early).
After 48 hours, contact support: ByteCart files a claim with the carrier and resolves it within 7 business days with a replacement or a refund.

## Delivery options
At checkout you can choose a delivery address, a ByteCart parcel locker or a carrier pickup point. Parcels are kept at pickup points and lockers for 7 days before being returned to ByteCart; a returned-to-sender order is refunded automatically, minus the original shipping fee.
You can add delivery instructions (for example "leave with neighbour") for DHL and DPD shipments. Items over €500 always require a signature on delivery.
