# Gift Cards

## Buying gift cards
Digital gift cards are available for any amount from €10 to €500 and are delivered by email within 15 minutes. You can schedule delivery for a date up to 12 months ahead and add a personal message.
Physical gift cards in a greeting card are available for €25, €50 or €100 and are shipped free of charge with standard delivery.
Gift cards cannot be bought with another gift card or with a coupon.

## Validity and balance
ByteCart gift cards are valid for 3 years from the date of purchase.
To use a gift card, redeem the code in Account > Wallet; the balance is then linked to your account. You can check the remaining balance in Account > Wallet.

## Paying with a gift card
Gift card balance can be used for items sold by ByteCart and by marketplace sellers, but not for ByteCart Plus membership fees.
Up to 5 gift cards can be used on one order. If the balance does not cover the full amount, the rest can be paid by card, PayPal, Apple Pay or Google Pay (not Klarna).

## Refunds and returns
Gift cards cannot be returned, refunded or exchanged for cash (except where required by law).
When you return an item paid with a gift card, the amount is refunded to your gift card balance. If the original gift card has expired in the meantime, the refunded amount gets a new validity of 12 months.

## Lost or stolen gift cards
Treat a gift card code like cash. A code that has already been redeemed by someone else cannot be replaced.
If a code has not been redeemed yet, ByteCart can block it and issue a new code if you provide the proof of purchase (order number or receipt).

## Corporate gift cards
Companies can order gift cards in bulk for employees or customers. Bulk orders start at €1,000 in total and can be paid by invoice (30 days) after a credit check.
Corporate orders over €5,000 receive a 3% discount on the gift card value. Request an offer via the "Corporate gifts" form; the business team answers within 2 business days.
