# Privacy and Data

## Data we collect
ByteCart processes the data needed to run your account and orders: name, addresses, email, phone number, order history, payment status (not full card numbers) and support conversations.
Card details are processed and stored by ByteCart's PCI DSS certified payment provider; ByteCart itself never stores full card numbers or CVC codes.
ByteCart is the data controller and follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## Your rights
You have the right to access, correct, delete and export your personal data, and to object to marketing.
The quickest way to get a copy of your data is Settings > Privacy > "Download my data": ByteCart prepares a JSON and CSV export within 48 hours and sends a download link that is valid for 7 days.
Formal requests to the Data Protection Officer (privacy@bytecart.example) are answered within 30 days; for complex requests this can be extended by up to 2 months, in which case you are informed within the first 30 days.

## Deleting your data
Deleting your data means closing your account (see Account and Security). Account data is deleted within 30 days of closure.
Invoices and order records are kept for 10 years because tax law requires it; they are restricted and not used for marketing.

## Data retention
Support chat transcripts and emails are kept for 12 months. Conversations with the Support Copilot are kept for 90 days for quality assurance and are not used to train third-party AI models.
Browsing data used for recommendations is kept for 13 months.

## Marketing and cookies
You can unsubscribe from marketing emails with the link in every email or in Settings > Notifications; the change takes effect within 48 hours. Order and security emails cannot be switched off.
Personalised recommendations can be turned off in Settings > Privacy.
Non-essential cookies (analytics and advertising) are only used with your consent, which you can change at any time via "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page.

## Sharing data with partners
ByteCart shares only the data needed to fulfil an order: the carrier receives your name, delivery address and phone number; marketplace sellers receive the name and delivery address for the items they ship; Klarna and PayPal receive the data needed for the payment.
ByteCart does not sell personal data to third parties.
