# Returns and Refunds

## Return window
How long do you have to return an item, and can you still return it? Items sold by ByteCart can be returned within 30 days of the delivery date for any reason ("change of mind"). The return is on time if it is registered in the account within the window; the parcel must then be handed to the carrier within 14 days of registering the return.
If you are a ByteCart Plus member, you get an extended 60-day return window on items sold by ByteCart. Exception: electronics always keep the standard 30-day return window, also for Plus members.
Holiday extension: items delivered between 15 November and 24 December can be returned until 31 January of the following year.
The Plus benefit depends on the membership status at the time the order was placed (shown as the customer tier on the order), not on the status when the return is requested.
Items sold by marketplace sellers follow the seller's own return policy (see Marketplace Sellers).

## Condition of returned items
Returned items must be unused, complete and in their original packaging. Fashion items must still have their tags attached.
The following items cannot be returned for change of mind: beauty and cosmetic products whose hygiene seal has been opened, underwear and swimwear with the hygiene liner removed, personalised or engraved items, digital codes, gift cards and perishable food.
Books can be returned if they are undamaged, even if they have been opened.
Faulty, damaged or wrong items are never subject to these restrictions (see Warranty and Damaged Items).

## How to start a return
Go to Account > Orders, select the order and choose "Return item". Select the item(s) and a reason.
For items sold by ByteCart you receive a free return label as a QR code by email. Drop the parcel off at any DHL or Hermes parcel shop, or at a ByteCart parcel locker.
Bulky items over 20 kg (for example a desk, sofa or wardrobe) do not need to be sent back by parcel: they are collected from your address: ByteCart schedules a free courier pickup within 5 business days of registering the return.
Keep the drop-off receipt until the refund has been completed.

## Return shipping costs
Return shipping is free for items sold by ByteCart.
Exception: a change-of-mind return from an order with a total under €25 costs a €3.95 return fee, which is deducted from the refund. ByteCart Plus members never pay return fees.
There is never a return fee when the item is faulty, damaged or not what you ordered.

## Refund timing
ByteCart issues the refund within 5 business days after the returned item arrives at the ByteCart warehouse and passes inspection.
How long the money takes to appear depends on the payment method:
- Card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express): 3–5 business days after the refund is issued.
- PayPal: within 24 hours after the refund is issued.
- Klarna: the Klarna invoice is adjusted within 2 business days.
- Gift card: refunded to the ByteCart gift card balance immediately; it cannot be paid out in cash.
Original outbound shipping fees are refunded only if the entire order is returned, or if the item was faulty, damaged or wrong.

## Refund status
You can follow the status of a return in Account > Orders: "Return registered", "In transit to warehouse", "Received", "Refund issued".
Card refunds take 3–5 business days to appear after the refund is issued, PayPal refunds within 24 hours.
If you still have not received the money 10 business days after the status changed to "Refund issued", contact support with your order number and ByteCart will trace the payment with the payment provider.

## Exchanges
ByteCart does not offer direct exchanges: return the item and place a new order.
Exception: fashion items sold by ByteCart can be exchanged once for a different size free of charge within the return window, using "Exchange size" in Account > Orders. The replacement size is shipped as soon as the original item has been scanned by the carrier.

## Returns of bundles and free gifts
Items bought as a bundle (for example a console with a game) must be returned complete; returning only part of a bundle refunds the bundle price minus the regular price of the items you keep.
If an order included a free gift with purchase, the gift must be returned together with the item that qualified for it; otherwise its regular price is deducted from the refund.
