# Promotions and Coupons

## Using coupons
Only one coupon code can be used per order. Enter it in the "Coupon" field at checkout; a coupon cannot be added after the order has been placed.
Coupons apply to items sold by ByteCart only. They cannot be used for gift cards, ByteCart Plus membership fees, marketplace items or ByteCart Care.
A coupon can be combined with sale prices, with ByteCart credit and with gift cards, but not with another coupon: you cannot combine or stack two coupons (for example WELCOME10 and the newsletter coupon) on one order.

## Welcome coupon
New customers receive the coupon WELCOME10 after creating an account: 10% off the first order, with a minimum order value of €30 and a maximum discount of €20.
WELCOME10 is valid for 30 days after sign-up and can be used once per customer.

## Newsletter coupon
Subscribing to the ByteCart newsletter gives a one-time €5 coupon for orders of €50 or more, valid for 60 days.
Unsubscribing does not cancel a coupon you already received.

## Price drop protection
If the price of an item sold by ByteCart drops after you bought it (it now costs less than you paid), ByteCart refunds the difference as ByteCart credit: within 7 days of purchase for standard customers and within 14 days for ByteCart Plus members.
Request it in Account > Orders > "Price drop". It does not apply to flash deals, Black Friday week prices, marketplace items or prices from other shops (ByteCart does not price-match competitors).

## Coupons and returns
If you return part of an order that used a coupon, the discount is split across the items in proportion to their price, and you are refunded the amount actually paid for the returned item.
The coupon is not reissued, unless the entire order is returned while the coupon would still have been valid.

## Sales events
ByteCart runs seasonal sales (for example Black Friday week and summer sale). ByteCart Plus members get 24-hour early access.
Flash deals are available in limited quantities and while the timer runs; ByteCart does not offer rain checks for sold-out deals.

## ByteCart credit
ByteCart credit comes from delay vouchers, Plus cashback, price drop refunds and goodwill gestures. It is used automatically at checkout (you can switch this off), is valid for 12 months from the date it is issued, cannot be transferred to another account and cannot be paid out in cash.

## Refer a friend
Share your personal referral link from Account > Refer a friend. When a new customer places a first order of €40 or more through your link, you both receive €10 ByteCart credit once the order has been delivered and the return window has passed.
You can earn referral credit for up to 10 friends per calendar year. Referral credit cannot be combined with WELCOME10 on the friend's first order.
