# Warranty and Damaged Items

## Damaged on arrival
If an item sold by ByteCart arrived damaged (broken, cracked, dented or leaking), report it within 7 days of delivery in Account > Orders > "Report a problem", with photos of the item and the packaging.
You can choose a free replacement (if in stock) or a full refund, including the original shipping fee.
For damaged items with a price under €30 you do not need to send the item back. For items of €30 or more ByteCart sends a free return label, or arranges a free pickup for bulky items.
Damage reported after 7 days is handled as a warranty claim (see Legal guarantee).

## Wrong or missing items
Received the wrong item (for example the wrong size or colour, or a different product than you ordered) or is an item missing? Report a wrong or missing item within 14 days of delivery.
Before reporting a missing item, check whether the order was sent in several parcels: each parcel has its own tracking ID in Account > Orders.
ByteCart ships a missing or correct item free of charge with express delivery within 2 business days of your report. Return the wrong item with the free return label within 14 days; if it is not returned, it is charged to you.

## Legal guarantee
All new items have a 2-year legal guarantee from the delivery date. During the first 12 months a defect is presumed to have existed at delivery; after 12 months you may be asked to show that the defect was not caused by misuse.
The guarantee does not cover normal wear and tear, accidental damage (drops, liquid) or damage caused by unauthorised repairs.
ByteCart Renewed (refurbished) items come with a 12-month guarantee.

## Faulty electronics
If an electronics item sold by ByteCart (for example headphones, a laptop or a smartwatch) has stopped working or developed a fault within 30 days of delivery, you get a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.
If it stops working after 30 days (for example after a few months), ByteCart first tries to repair the item; repairs normally take 10–15 business days and shipping to and from the repair centre is free. If the same fault cannot be fixed after 2 repair attempts, or if a repair takes longer than 30 days, you get a replacement or a full refund.
Start a warranty claim in Account > Orders > "Report a problem" and keep the original invoice.

## ByteCart Care extended warranty
ByteCart Care adds 2 extra years of protection against defects after the 2-year legal guarantee ends (4 years in total).
You can add it to electronics sold by ByteCart (for example a laptop or phone) at checkout or up to 30 days after delivery. It costs 12% of the item price (minimum €9).
ByteCart Care covers defects only, not accidental damage or loss. It can be cancelled for a full refund within 30 days of purchase if no claim has been made.

## Product safety recalls
If a product you bought is recalled by the manufacturer, ByteCart emails all affected customers with instructions. Stop using the product immediately.
Recalled items can be returned for a full refund at any time during the recall, regardless of the return window, and without a return fee.
