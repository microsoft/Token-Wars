# Payments and Billing

## Accepted payment methods
Payment options you can use: ByteCart accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Klarna and ByteCart gift cards.
ByteCart does not accept cash on delivery, bank transfer, cheques or cryptocurrencies.
All prices are shown in euros (EUR) and include VAT. ByteCart does not charge currency conversion fees, but a bank outside the euro zone may.

## When you are charged
For card, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments the amount is authorised when the order is placed and charged when the order is shipped. If an order is shipped in several parcels, each parcel is charged separately when it ships.
PayPal payments are charged immediately when the order is placed.
If an order is cancelled before it ships, the card authorisation hold is released by your bank, usually within 7 days (up to 10 days for some banks). ByteCart cannot speed this up.

## Klarna
Klarna offers two options at checkout: "Pay in 30 days" (available for all orders) and "Pay in 3" interest-free instalments (for orders between €50 and €1,500).
Klarna manages the invoices and reminders; late fees are set by Klarna, not ByteCart.
When you register a return, Klarna pauses the payment for the returned amount until the refund is processed, so you do not need to pay for items you are sending back.
Klarna cannot be combined with a gift card in the same order.

## Invoices and VAT
The invoice (PDF) is available in Account > Orders 24 hours after the order ships.
Business customers must add their company name and VAT ID in Account > Settings before placing the order. A VAT ID cannot be added to an invoice after it has been issued.
Other invoice corrections (for example a billing address typo) can be requested from support within 30 days of the invoice date.

## Failed payments and double charges
If a payment fails, the order stays in "payment pending" for 24 hours so you can retry with another method; after 24 hours it is cancelled automatically.
Seeing the same amount twice is almost always a temporary authorisation hold, which disappears within 7 days.
If two completed charges are still visible after 7 days, contact support with a screenshot of your bank statement; ByteCart refunds a confirmed duplicate charge within 3 business days.
ByteCart never asks for your full card number, CVC or PayPal password by email, chat or phone.

## Refunds to the original payment method
Refunds are always paid back to the payment method used for the order. If a card has expired or been replaced, the bank normally forwards the refund to the new card automatically.
If the original payment method is closed (for example a closed PayPal account), support can issue the refund as ByteCart credit or, on request, by bank transfer within 10 business days.

## Payment security
Card payments are protected by 3-D Secure: your bank may ask you to confirm the payment in its app or with a one-time code. If the confirmation fails or times out after 10 minutes, the payment is declined and you can try again.
For security reasons, ByteCart may ask for additional verification on large orders (over €1,000) or when a new delivery address is used together with a new card.
