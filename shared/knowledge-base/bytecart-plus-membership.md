# ByteCart Plus Membership

## Price and free trial
ByteCart Plus costs €59 per year or €6.99 per month.
New members can try Plus free for 30 days. The free trial is available once per customer account and payment method; after the trial the chosen plan is charged automatically unless it is cancelled before the trial ends.

## Plus benefits
- Free standard delivery on all items sold by ByteCart, with no minimum order value.
- Express delivery for €4.99 instead of €9.99.
- 60-day return window on items sold by ByteCart (electronics stay at 30 days).
- No return fees.
- Price drop protection for 14 days after purchase (7 days for standard customers).
- 24-hour early access to sales events such as Black Friday.
- 2% cashback as ByteCart credit on items sold by ByteCart, credited 30 days after delivery, up to €100 per membership year.
- Priority support: email replies within 4 hours during support hours.

## Which orders get Plus benefits
Plus benefits apply to orders placed while the membership is active. The tier at the moment of ordering is shown on each order ("plus" or "standard"). Joining Plus later does not add benefits to earlier orders, and cancelling Plus does not remove benefits from orders placed while you were a member.
Plus benefits apply only to items sold by ByteCart. Items sold by marketplace sellers keep the seller's shipping fees and return policy.

## Sharing Plus
A Plus member can share the benefits with one additional adult living at the same address (household sharing). Invite them in Account > Plus > Household. The shared member uses their own account and payment method.

## Cancelling Plus
You can cancel at any time in Account > Plus.
Monthly plans end at the end of the current monthly period; there is no refund for the current month.
Annual plans can be refunded in full if you cancel within 14 days of the annual charge and have not used any Plus benefit since that charge. Otherwise there is no refund, and the membership stays active until the end of the paid year.
Cashback that has already been credited stays in your account after cancelling.

## Student plan
Verified students can join ByteCart Plus for €29 per year. Student status is verified once per year via a university email address or student ID.
The student plan includes all Plus benefits except household sharing, and can be used for a maximum of 4 years.
