# Account and Security

## Creating and closing an account
You must be at least 18 years old to create a ByteCart account. One account per person; the email address is your login.
You can close your account in Settings > Privacy > Close account. The account is closed within 30 days. An account cannot be closed while it has open orders or open returns.
Any remaining gift card balance and ByteCart credit is forfeited when the account is closed, so use it or transfer it to another payment first — ByteCart shows a warning before you confirm.

## Passwords and sign-in
Passwords must have at least 12 characters. Two-step verification (2FA) can be enabled with an authenticator app or SMS in Settings > Security, and ByteCart strongly recommends it.
Password reset links are valid for 60 minutes.
After 5 failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes. You can unlock it immediately by resetting your password.

## Changing email address or phone number
Changing the email address requires a verification code sent to both the old and the new address. Codes are valid for 15 minutes.
If you no longer have access to the old email address, support can verify your identity with the last 4 digits of the card used on a recent order and the delivery address of that order.

## Hacked or compromised accounts
ByteCart never asks for your password, 2FA codes or full card details by email, SMS or phone.
If your account was hacked, someone placed an order you did not make, or you think your account has been compromised: reset your password immediately, sign out of all devices in Settings > Security, and contact support.
Orders you did not place are cancelled if they are still "processing". Unauthorised purchases reported within 30 days are refunded in full after investigation, including gift card balance that was spent without your permission.

## Customer service opening hours and contact channels
The Support Copilot chat is available 24/7.
Human agents are available by chat from 08:00 to 20:00 CET, Monday to Saturday.
Phone support (+49 30 1234 5678) is available from 09:00 to 18:00 CET, Monday to Friday.
Email support (support@bytecart.example) answers emails within 24 hours; ByteCart Plus members get priority replies within 4 hours during support hours.

## Addresses and order history
You can save up to 10 delivery addresses in Account > Addresses. The default address is preselected at checkout.
Order history for the last 10 years is available in Account > Orders; orders older than 2 years are shown without product images.
